## About HaorShield

**HaorShield** is an AI-assisted flood and agricultural intelligence platform developed to help communities in Bangladesh's haor regions better understand potential flash-flood concerns and their implications for agriculture.

The platform brings together **weather forecasts, rainfall information, satellite-based flood research, geospatial analysis, and agricultural guidance** in one accessible website.

Our goal is to make complex environmental information easier to understand and support better preparedness among farmers, local communities, and researchers.

## Key Features

- 🌊 **Flood Risk Screening:** Explore experimental flood-concern outlooks for the next 24, 48, and 72 hours, with location-based geographic information.
- 🌦️ **Weather Forecasts:** View temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind conditions, and upcoming weather.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Rain Map:** Explore geographically visualized rainfall conditions across supported locations.
- 🌾 **Land & Cropland Outlook:** Examine mapped land and agricultural coverage alongside experimental flood-screening signals.
- 🚜 **Crop Advice:** Receive agricultural preparedness and recovery guidance based on crop type, growth stage, and field condition.
- 📅 **Historical Flood Explorer:** Explore historical flood events and their geographic relationship with agricultural land.

## How HaorShield Works

HaorShield combines environmental forecasts with geographic and historical research data to provide accessible flood-related information.

Its research incorporates technologies and datasets such as:

- **Satellite imagery:** Sentinel-1 SAR and MODIS
- **Weather and rainfall:** Open-Meteo and CHIRPS
- **River conditions:** Copernicus GloFAS
- **Agricultural land cover:** ESA WorldCover
- **Geospatial analysis:** Geographic boundaries, research grids, and interactive maps
- **Machine learning:** Experimental historical flood-exposure and susceptibility research

These sources help investigate environmental conditions, historical flood patterns, and agricultural exposure.
